Three Questions Worth Asking Before You Decide Anything

Calculators give you realistic ranges, while actual carrier payouts vary by state, age, and policy structure.

  • 1. Risk Comfort: If the market repeated a 2008- or 2022-style drop next year, how much of your current savings would you feel comfortable losing?
  • 2. Recovery Timeline: If that drop happened right as you needed income, how many years would it push back your retirement plans?
  • 3. Clarity on Paper: Would it be helpful to see live carrier quotes for your exact age and state side-by-side—with zero pressure to move anything?
